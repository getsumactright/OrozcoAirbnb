Straight about the area

The part most listings leave out.

Golden Hill is genuinely central, and genuinely urban. Our own block is quiet and residential — and, as in much of central San Diego, you will sometimes pass someone sleeping rough on the streets nearby. It rarely amounts to more than that, and guests consistently tell me it didn’t affect their stay.

I would rather you hear it from me than arrive and wonder why nobody mentioned it. The house is fenced and private, the entry is keyless, and your parking is on the lot.

Elijah· Superhost for 8 years
